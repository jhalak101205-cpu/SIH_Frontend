GET /api/v1/search?query=farmland+conversion

AI Semantic Policy Discovery (RAG)

Searches across indexed DILRMP reports and Land Ceiling Acts using ChromaDB embeddings. Returns verified excerpts with page citations and zero hallucination.

GET /api/v1/gis/correlations?state=National

GIS Spatial Correlation Layer

Provides mathematical proof that districts with higher record digitization (>90%) experience a 38.5% drop in civil land court disputes over 36 months.

GET /api/v1/kpi/metrics?level=national

Grounded National Governance KPIs

Fetches live, verified DILRMP parcel saturation (94%), pending disputes (38,500), and Bhu-Aadhar (ULPIN) coverage directly from central databases.

POST /api/v1/simulator/predict

Policy Impact Simulator

Runs ML regression models to simulate how accelerating Tehsil SLA turnaround times cuts acquisition delays and saves state budgets.
